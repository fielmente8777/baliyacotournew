We're setting up the **Create Your Own Design** section of the website, where customers build their outfit step by step: choose the garment, fabric, colour, neckline, embroidery, sleeves and so on, then add their measurements.

To set it up, we need the details below. Please put everything in one Google Drive folder named **"Design Your Own"**, shared as "Anyone with the link can view". Two sheets are attached to make it easier (one row per item). If you prefer, a simple list in Word or WhatsApp works too.

**1. THE GARMENTS**
Sheet: design-builder-garments · Folder: Design Your Own › 1 Garments

Which garments should customers be able to design? For example Kurti, Anarkali, Patiala Suit, Sharara Set, Lehenga.

For each garment, please send:
- **Name** of the garment
- **Starting price** in ₹: the price before any extras are added
- **One line** describing it, e.g. "A flared, floor-length Anarkali for festive days"
- **The steps** the customer goes through, in order. For example: Fabric › Colour › Neckline › Embroidery › Sleeves › Sleeve Cuff › Bottom (Salwar / Pants) › Dupatta › Length › Size. Not every garment needs every step.
- **Which steps are compulsory**, e.g. Fabric, Colour and Size must be chosen, but Embroidery is optional
- **Two photos of the garment:**
  - **Card photo:** shown on the "What would you like to design?" screen. Portrait, attractive, can be styled.
  - **Preview photo:** the base picture customers see while designing. This one is important:
    - Front view, on a mannequin or flat lay, the **whole garment in frame**
    - **Plain, light-coloured garment with no embroidery or print** (white, ivory or a pastel works best; we change the colour on screen)
    - Plain background in a different shade from the garment, e.g. a light grey backdrop for an ivory kurta
    - Portrait (3:4), at least 1200 × 1600 px, no watermark or text

**2. THE CHOICES IN EACH STEP**
Sheet: design-builder-options · Folder: Design Your Own › 2 Choices › (one sub-folder per step, e.g. "Fabric", "Neckline")

For **every choice** in every step, please send:
- **Name**, e.g. Chanderi, V Neck, Full Sleeve
- **Extra price** in ₹ (write 0 if it's included in the starting price)
- **A photo or illustration**: square, at least 800 × 800 px, plain background, the choice clearly visible
- **One short line** about it (optional), e.g. "Light, sheer weave with a subtle shine"
- **If it's only for some garments**, say which, e.g. "Sweetheart neck: Anarkali and Sharara only"

What we need for each step:

- **Fabric:** every fabric you offer, with a close-up photo of the weave or texture (a folded swatch in good daylight is perfect).
- **Colour:** every colour, as a name plus **colour code** (e.g. Wine #722F37). If you don't have codes, send a clear daylight photo of each fabric shade and we'll match it. Mention if any colour isn't available in certain fabrics.
- **Neckline:** each neck style. A simple front sketch or photo of the neckline is ideal.
- **Embroidery:** each embroidery design, with:
  - a close-up photo of the work, plus a photo of it on a garment if you have one
  - **which necklines it suits**, e.g. "Zari V Border: V Neck only" (customers only see embroidery that matches their chosen neck)
  - the extra price
- **Sleeves:** each sleeve type (sleeveless, short, three-quarter, full…).
- **Sleeve cuff** (if offered): cuff styles, and whether they apply only to full sleeves.
- **Bottom / lower:** salwar, Patiala, straight pants, palazzo, sharara…, and which garments each goes with.
- **Dupatta:** each dupatta option (including "No dupatta"), with a photo and price.
- **Length:** the length options (hip, knee, calf, floor…) and which garments they apply to.
- **Size:**
  - the ready sizes you offer (XS to XXL…) **and your size chart**: bust, waist, hip and length for each size, in inches
  - whether "**Custom measurements**" should be offered too
