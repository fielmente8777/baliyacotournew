# Home page and product photos: prompts

Ready-to-paste prompts for **Google Gemini (Nano Banana)**, **Canva AI** (Magic Media, *Photo* style) or **Freepik AI Image Generator**, plus Freepik search terms where a stock photo is the better choice. Prompts for the Design Builder option cards are in `option-image-prompts.md`.

Checked against the live store on 30 Oct 2026: **12 products, 10 of them with no photo**, so the website shows a placeholder for those.

## Ground rules

- **Real people stay real.** The founders' photo, the customer gallery and the reviews must be real photos and real words. Don't generate them: an invented founder or customer misleads buyers. The reviews section is switched off until real ones are added.
- **Behind the scenes:** a real photo of your own workshop is always better. If you use stock or AI images for now, keep the captions general ("Hand embroidery"), and replace them when you can.
- **Products:** an AI picture must show the garment as it will really be made: same colour, fabric, cut and embroidery. Check each one against the actual piece before publishing.
- Make 3–4 versions of each prompt and keep the best. For a matching set, do them in one Gemini chat and attach your first approved image with *"Same model, backdrop and lighting as the attached image."*

## Part 2: Products (Shopify)

Upload in **Shopify admin → Products → (product) → Media → Add**. The first picture is the one on the product card. The site shows product photos at **3:4 portrait**: make them at least **2000 × 2667** so the zoom stays sharp.

### For every product

1. Paste the product's **main prompt** below into a new Gemini chat. Keep the best result: that's photo 1.
2. In the same chat, attach photo 1 and send each of these, one at a time, for photos 2–4:

```text
Same model, same outfit, same backdrop and lighting as the attached image. Back view, full length, standing straight, showing the back of the garment clearly. 3:4 portrait.
```

```text
Same outfit as the attached image. Close-up detail shot of the neckline and the embroidery or print, showing the fabric texture and stitching sharply. No face. 3:4 portrait.
```

```text
Same model and same outfit as the attached image, now in a styled setting: a warm, softly lit Indian home courtyard with plants and a carved wooden door, natural relaxed pose, three-quarter view. 3:4 portrait, photorealistic.
```

To keep all 12 products looking like one shoot, attach your first approved product photo when you start each new product and add: *"Same model, backdrop and lighting as the attached image, but wearing:"* before the main prompt.

### Getting the list from Sidekick next time

When products are added, ask **Shopify Sidekick**:

```text
List every product in my store as a table with: title, handle, product type, tags, number of images, and the first 200 characters of the description. Sort by product type.
```

Then write a main prompt for each new product in the same pattern: model and pose, then *colour + fabric + cut + embroidery or print + bottom wear + dupatta*, then the backdrop line.
